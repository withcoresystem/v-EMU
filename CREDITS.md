## Upstream projects

v-EMU builds on QEMU/KVM, libvirt, FreeRDP, OVMF/EDK2, noVNC, PySide6, Inter,
JetBrains Mono and Clarity icons, among other open-source components. The full
third-party notices and license texts are in [`NOTICE`](NOTICE),
[`firmware/NOTICE`](firmware/NOTICE) and the component license files.

## Forking

The 0.3.0 tree is the broad public fork base. Forks should keep `LICENSE`,
`NOTICE` and this `CREDITS.md`, state their changes clearly, and preserve the
upstream project credit.
